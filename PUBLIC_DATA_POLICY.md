# Public Data Policy

This portfolio repository is intentionally separated from the private development repository.

Only synthetic or explicitly redistributable material may be published here. The following are prohibited:

- employer knowledge bases and internal operating documents;
- customer proposal content or submitted packages;
- confidential pricing or commercial terms;
- solicitation files without confirmed redistribution rights;
- personal, regulated, or confidential information;
- credentials, tokens, connection strings, or production endpoints;
- production Opportunity Records and generated artifacts; and
- distinctive model inputs or outputs derived from prohibited material.

Public demonstrations will use fictional organizations, fictional contacts, reserved domains such as `example.invalid`, and synthetic RFx fixtures designed to exercise the same workflow behavior without reproducing confidential content.
